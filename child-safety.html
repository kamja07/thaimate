1. 금지 행위

ThaiMate Child Safety Standards

Effective: June 25, 2026 · Operated by ThaiMate

ThaiMate ("the Service") maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). These standards explain how the Service protects children, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Prohibited Conduct

  • Posting, sharing, transmitting, soliciting or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation, or any related material (CSAM)
  • Grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any sexual approach toward minors
  • Any text, image, link or behavior that sexualizes or endangers a child

Such conduct results in immediate permanent account termination and content removal, and is reported to authorities as required by law.

2. In-App Reporting

Users can report child-safety concerns at any time via:

  • Reporting posts, profiles and chats using the in-app report/block controls on content and users
  • Email: kamja07@gmail.com (please mark the subject "Child Safety")

Reports are reviewed promptly; confirmed violations lead to content removal, account suspension and reporting to authorities.

3. Legal Compliance & Reporting to Authorities

The Service complies with all applicable child-protection laws in the regions where it operates (including Thailand and South Korea). Where CSAM is identified, we preserve and remove the content and report it to local and national authorities and to relevant bodies such as NCMEC, as required by law.

4. Child Safety Point of Contact

Designated contact for CSAM-prevention practices and compliance:

  • Email: kamja07@gmail.com

5. Changes

If these standards change, we will post updates on this page.
